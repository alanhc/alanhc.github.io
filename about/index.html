      Summary

      Embedded software engineer who uses AI agents to speed up firmware development. Register-level work is on the server side: C/C++ drivers over I2C/PMBus, device tree, Yocto builds, and the D-Bus/systemd services above them, plus cross-machine debugging of production BMCs with gdbserver, live-attach and core dump analysis — a workflow I presented at COSCUP 2025. On Android platform work the leverage has been automation: agent skills that take the repetitive half of the job off the critical path. Three platform stacks in two years — rack provisioning, OpenBMC firmware, Android platform — each with its own toolchain and debugger. Getting productive on unfamiliar hardware quickly is the part I’d point at. Also comfortable with performance profiling (perf, uftrace) and IPMI/Redfish protocol stacks, and interested in open-source work and cross-domain collaboration.
